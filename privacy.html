SayGato Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

App: SayGato. Developer: Carlos Morales.

The short version

SayGato does not collect, send, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. Everything you do in the app is saved on your device, not on our servers.

What data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, age, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It does not use your microphone, camera, or location. It does not connect to our servers or anyone else’s to send your information. We have no servers for the app, and we cannot see what you do in it.

What is stored on your device

So the app can remember where you left off, it saves a small amount of information only on your device:

  • which direction you are learning (English to Spanish, or Spanish to English) and other app settings, including whether sound is on
  • your progress in the games and mysteries, and review timing for words
  • the phrases in your My Words list
  • your in-game coins and the clothes you picked for Churro (coins are not money and cannot be bought)

This information never leaves the app to reach us, and we cannot see it.

On Android, SayGato turns off the system’s app backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account or to a new phone. We cannot see your progress, and we never receive a copy of it.

On iPhone or iPad, your device’s own iCloud or computer backup may include this app’s data. We can’t see those backups.

Voices and audio

The voices in this app are computer-generated with Microsoft Azure AI Speech. The spoken lines are recorded ahead of time and bundled inside the app, so no speech and no other user data is sent to Azure — or to anyone else — when you play.

If a recorded clip can’t play, the app uses your device’s own text-to-speech voice instead. Your phone or tablet’s speech settings are controlled by Apple or Google, not by SayGato. The app does not use the microphone.

Permissions

SayGato does not ask for access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location. On Android, the app includes a few standard system permissions used for sound and vibration. It also includes an internet permission that comes with the tools used to build the app. Even so, the app does not send your information anywhere.

How to delete your data

  • Uninstall the app. This deletes everything SayGato saved on that device.
  • Or clear the app’s data: on Android, go to Settings > Apps > SayGato > Storage > Clear storage. On iPhone or iPad, deleting the app removes its data.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

On iPhone or iPad, your device’s own iCloud or computer backup may include this app’s data. We can’t see those backups.

Families and young people

SayGato is a general family app for all ages. It is not specifically directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Third parties

SayGato does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect any. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. The voices were generated with Microsoft Azure AI Speech before the app was built, and those recordings ship inside the app; Azure does not receive data from people using the app.

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Those stores have their own privacy policies for things like downloads and payments.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting any data, we will say so clearly in this policy and in the app store listings before that change ships.
