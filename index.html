SayGato. Say more.

Spanish and English word games with Churro, the orange cat. For beginners and the whole family. You already know more than you think.

Coming to iPhone, iPad and Android

Play your way into both languages

  • Clue Mysteries. Follow the clues and crack the case in both languages.
  • Where does Churro go? Put Churro in the right spot and pick up words along the way.
  • Cat Café Orders. Take orders and practice everyday phrases.
  • Vocab Quiz, Churro dice and Sentences. Quick rounds that help words stick, and real sentences you can use.
  • My Words. Every word you’ve learned, in one place.
  • Cat Closet. Earn coins by playing and dress Churro in 12 colors and a diamantina finish. Milestone gifts unlock as you keep playing, and they can’t be bought.

Both directions

For English speakers learning Spanish and Spanish speakers learning English. Chapters cover everyday situations like the café, the market, the bus, telling time, the weather and weekend plans, at a beginner pace.

Watch out for Sneaky Words: éxito means success, not exit.

Simple and private

No account, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Coins come only from playing. Your progress is saved on your device. We don’t collect your data.

Read the privacy policy for the full details.

Voices

The voices in this app are computer-generated with Microsoft Azure AI Speech.

The spoken lines are recorded ahead of time and come bundled in the app, so nothing you hear or do is sent to Azure. If a clip can’t play, the app uses your device’s own voice instead. SayGato does not use the microphone.
