Need help, found a mistake, or have a question? Email Carlos Morales at saygatoapp@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There’s no sign-in.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Coins in the Cat Closet are earned only by playing, and they are not money.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. We don’t receive it. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage on Android, deletes your progress. Progress can’t move to a new phone yet. On Android, the app is not included in your phone’s backups. On iPhone or iPad, your device’s own iCloud or computer backup may include this app’s data. We can’t see those backups.
Can I hear the lines out loud?
Yes. The voices in this app are computer-generated with Microsoft Azure AI Speech. The recordings are made ahead of time and bundled in the app, so nothing is sent to Azure when you play. If a clip can’t play, the app uses your device’s own voice. You can turn sound on or off with the Sound button. SayGato does not use the microphone.
Can I learn English instead of Spanish?
Yes. Switch the direction in the app: English to Spanish, or Spanish to English.
What if a word or a voice clip seems wrong?
Please write to us at saygatoapp@gmail.com and tell us which game, which line, and which language you were using.

You can also read the privacy policy.
